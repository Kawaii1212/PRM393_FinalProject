# Privacy Policy for Gear Sports

**Last updated:** November 2, 2026

Welcome to **Gear Sports** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

* **Location Data (GPS):**  
  Gear Sports collects precise and approximate location information while you use the app to:
  - Display your current location on the map.
  - Calculate distances to nearby sports and badminton shops.
  - Filter sports stores within your selected search radius (e.g., 1km, 2km, 5km, 10km).  
  *We do not track your location in the background when the app is closed.*

* **Account & Authentication Information:**  
  When you register or log in, we collect your email address and display name through Google Firebase Authentication to authenticate your session and maintain your profile.

* **User Content & Communications:**  
  - **Racket Garage & Equipment Data:** Information about your rackets, string tension, string types, and restringing schedules.
  - **In-App Chat:** Messages, consultation requests, and photos sent between customers and shop owners are stored securely in Google Cloud Firestore and Firebase Storage.

* **Device & Technical Information:**  
  We may collect anonymous device information (such as operating system version and hardware model) to assist with error diagnostics and performance improvements.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:
- Providing the core features of the app (store locator, stringing booking, racket management, chat).
- Facilitating real-time communication between customers and sports store managers.
- Ensuring account security and preventing unauthorized access.

---

### 3. Data Storage & Third-Party Services

Gear Sports utilizes industry-standard Google Cloud / Firebase services:
- **Firebase Authentication** (Identity management)
- **Cloud Firestore & Storage** (Database and media storage)
- **OpenStreetMap** (Map tile rendering)

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

---

### 4. Permissions Used

* `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` & `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`: To find and show nearby sports stores.
* `INTERNET`: To sync data with cloud database and load map tiles.
* `CAMERA` & `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`: To allow you to take and upload photos of rackets or send chat images.

---

### 5. Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.
